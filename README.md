## 📖 About the Game

A strategic RNG gacha & auto-battler based directly on Pratham's handwritten **Game Book**!

### Core Game Loop
1. **First You Roll**: Test your luck with RNG odds to roll across the entire **750 Beings** hierarchy.
2. **Then You Fight**: Take your rolled being straight into the battle arena to conquer dungeon foes and mythical beasts!
